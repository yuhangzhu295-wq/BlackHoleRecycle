### The T1 root cause, since it took three attempts
The gate absorbed one target and immediately asserted `mass > massBefore`, but
`CompressionSystem` **buffers** absorbed mass (`countThreshold 3` /
`massThreshold 180`) and only injects it at COLLECTING after
COMPRESSING+EJECTING. So when the preceding calibration left `bufferCount` at 0,
the mass legitimately had not been awarded yet. The flake was the harness
asserting a by-design asynchronous behaviour, not a gameplay defect. The fix
primes the buffer with **real CDP touch** until the target absorption is the
third item. **A lead of mine was wrong and is recorded in the commit**: the
payload's `touchDiagnostic.accepted: false` at `phase: 'END'` only means the
finger was released, not that input was rejected.
