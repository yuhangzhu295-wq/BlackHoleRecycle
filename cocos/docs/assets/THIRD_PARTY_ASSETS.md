## Policy

Only assets listed here may be imported from external sources. Original author and license remain attached to every local copy.

Rules carried over from the manifest and from the V4 asset direction:

- Every externally sourced asset must be listed in the manifest before it is imported.
- Unknown or unclear licensing is forbidden. Assets without an explicit commercial licence are not admitted.
- The original author and licence stay attached to every local copy.
- `.glb` files are imported through Cocos Creator only. No hand-edited `.prefab`, `.meta` or UUID values.
- Style target: low-poly casual cartoon. Primitive placeholders and photoreal high-poly assets are not admitted into the shipping look.
